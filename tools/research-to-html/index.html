Turn an industry question into a report people can actually read.

Research to HTML is a repository-scoped Codex skill for evidence-backed market and industry research. It keeps the research trail reviewable, then turns the approved findings into separate English and Simplified-Chinese static pages with the same conclusions, caveats, and sources.

01

One decision artifact, three quality layers.

The workflow treats research, translation, and interface design as connected checks—not as three unrelated generation steps.

Traceable evidence

Current claims point to direct sources. Observations, inferences, unknowns, dates, geography, and confidence stay distinguishable.

Equivalent bilingual pages

English and Chinese pages share the same figures, causal claims, limitations, interactions, and source set.

Low reading pressure

The conclusion appears first. Clear hierarchy, reading controls, accessible interactions, print support, and responsive layouts reduce effort without hiding evidence.

Designed for decisions

Use it for market structure, product and platform comparisons, business-model questions, creator economics, or technical ecosystems where the answer must remain inspectable after the AI session ends.

02

From question to public report.

The skill follows a fixed evidence-to-publication sequence while adapting the analysis to the domain.

  1. Define the decision. Set the audience, scope, geography, research cutoff, operational terms, and exclusions.
  2. Build the evidence record. Prefer current primary sources, record caveats, and separate what is observed from what is inferred.
  3. Write before styling. Approve the Markdown findings before turning them into English and Chinese reader interfaces.
  4. Validate the public artifact. Check privacy, citations, locale parity, accessibility, responsive behavior, metadata, JavaScript, print, and the exact files intended for publication.

05

What the workflow does not pretend to know.

Reliable output depends on evidence quality, a defined decision, and review—not on the confidence of generated prose.

Revenue is not profit

The skill does not infer profit from pledges, GMV, traffic, downloads, or funding success.

Unknown stays unknown

Missing platform data, unaudited claims, stale examples, and causal uncertainty must remain visible in the report.

Publishing is a separate gate

Only an explicit reader-facing allowlist is deployed. Skills, scripts, notebooks, templates, credentials, and local configuration remain outside the Pages artifact.
