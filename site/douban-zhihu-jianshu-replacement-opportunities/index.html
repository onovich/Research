Platform strategy field study · 2026

Traditional content platforms

Can independent developers replace Douban, Zhihu, or Jianshu? Zhihu's 2025 revenue fell 23.6%, yet it still reports 953.9 million cumulative content items.

This report separates operating data, content stock, and app-store signals. The evidence does not support one person rebuilding a whole platform, but it can turn adjacent opportunities into falsifiable hypotheses.

01

The data do not support one-for-one replacement

We observe contraction, large accumulated state, and high operating load—not a proven “best startup direction.”

Can an indie developer replace them?

The current evidence cannot show that they can. Zhihu's 953.9 million content items establish a migration scale; Douban and Jianshu lack comparable operating denominators.

Is there a startup opportunity?

There are hypotheses to test, not proven opportunities. Object curation, private-community operations, creator-owned publishing, and moderation tools each have a named buyer hypothesis but no comparable payment data.

Why has nobody taken the whole market?

Public data do not measure the reason directly. Large content stock, the expense structure, and multi-homing research support three mechanisms; they remain inferences rather than established causes.

Recommended product sentence

For one expert or enthusiast community, maintain a trusted object database and automate one repeated compare, shortlist, review, submit, procure, or report job.

03

The data support three mechanisms, not a causal proof

There is no direct statistic for “why no one replaced them.” These are narrower inferences from public numbers.

953.9M · State
Observation: Zhihu approaches one billion cumulative content items; Douban adds object catalogs, ratings, and collections. Inference: replacement starts with lawful seed data and coordinated supply and demand, not interface work alone.
130.8% · Operating load
Observation: Zhihu operating expense exceeded gross profit; sales and marketing equaled 45.6% of revenue and R&D 19.1%. Inference: public-content products must measure acquisition, moderation, support, and engineering cost.
18–20× · Visible-footprint gap
Observation: Douban and Zhihu have about 18.3 and 20.0 times Jianshu's cumulative iOS rating count. Inference: the targets are not equal in observable footprint; ratings still cannot substitute for MAU.
Unknown · Causality
No cross-platform migration, willingness-to-pay, or Douban and Jianshu operating-cost dataset exists here, so these mechanisms cannot explain the full absence of a successor.
Broad community is not lightweight SaaS

Zhihu reported RMB 2.749B in 2025 revenue, RMB 195.2M GAAP net loss, RMB 1.252B sales and marketing expense, and RMB 525M R&D expense. Even at scale, the operating model is heavy.

Vibe coding helps with the shell

AI can compress CRUD, authentication, search UI, payments, notifications, admin tools, normalization, and moderation queues. It cannot create licensed data, credible contributors, norms, distribution, retention, or production judgment.

05

Six weeks to test the wedge

Earn the right to add a community layer by first proving a repeated job, trusted data, and return behavior.

Week 1

Choose the object and job

  • Interview 12 people in one niche.
  • Collect the objects, files, links, and spreadsheets they actually use.
  • Stop if there is no repeated compare, coordinate, or report job.
Week 2

Seed the corpus manually

  • Create 100 verified object records and a transparent data policy.
  • Use licensed, public-domain, first-party, or consented data.
  • Test whether five users return without a social feed.
Week 3

Recruit credible supply

  • Invite 10 domain contributors.
  • Ask each for one structured review, checklist, or comparison.
  • Agree attribution, ownership, export, and moderation rules.
Week 4

Sell the workflow

  • Offer a paid workspace, export, alert, shortlist, or report.
  • Continue only if three users or one organization commits budget.
  • Do not open public posting yet.
Week 5

Build one bounded product

  • One object model, one workflow, one permission model.
  • One export path and one audit trail.
  • Instrument successful jobs, support time, and failed records.
Week 6

Measure return behavior

  • Track second-week return, useful contributions, and moderation minutes.
  • Open sharing only when it improves acquisition without lowering quality.
  • Stop if founder repair consumes the margin.
Do not build first

A general public feed, an unauthorized Douban mirror, an ad-funded community, an AI content farm, open discussion in a sensitive domain, or a creator platform with publishing but no ownership or paid outcome.
